### Fixed

- **Traffic map shows live requests again.** Incoming requests from the live stream are drawn on the map again, stay in place when the window or sidebar is resized, and the map renders sharply on high-DPI screens. The header shows whether the live stream is connected, and it reconnects on its own after an error.
- **Request logs page layout.** The route filter dropdown opens aligned under its button, is wider, keeps its checkboxes in one column and shows full route names on hover. Empty date filters read "From" / "To" instead of the browser's date mask, and the calendar icon is no longer clipped.
- **No more content running off the right edge.** Wide tables now scroll inside their card instead of stretching the whole page, so filter bars and header buttons stay visible (Logs, Routes, Users, archived routes, organization and group details).
- **Native controls follow the dark theme** (date pickers, scrollbars).
- **Ctrl/⌘+K no longer opens two things on the Routes page.** It now only opens the global search; the routes shortcut sheet moved to `?`. The header hint shows `⌘K` on macOS and `Ctrl K` elsewhere.
- **Routes pagination showed an empty page-size selector** because its default (20) was not one of the offered sizes. It now defaults to 25.
